Why it exists

What’s left is invisible when you pay.

For anyone who keeps a fixed monthly budget for everyday UPI spending: food orders, quick-commerce runs, movies.

Behind a PIN

Seeing the balance means opening a UPI app and entering a PIN, so it happens weekly. In between, it’s a guess.

Split across apps

Payments go out through Google Pay, PhonePe and CRED, from different bank accounts. No one place shows the total.

Too much effort

Tracking by hand means copying payments from 2–3 apps into a spreadsheet. Tried once, dropped.

The result: ₹1,000–2,000 over budget each month, quietly taken from savings.

Privacy first

Your money stays on your phone.

  • No internet permission. The app cannot send anything anywhere.
  • No login, no account. Nothing to sign up for.
  • Only UPI debit SMS are read, on the phone. Personal messages and OTPs are ignored.
  • Message text is never stored. Only the amount, payee and time are saved.
  • Your data, your call. Export everything as CSV, or uninstall to delete it all.
  • Never ads, never selling data.
Install

Install in a few minutes.

Paisa Pet isn’t on the Play Store yet, so Android shows a few warnings for an app downloaded from the web. Here’s what to tap. The demo video shows the full install from start to finish.

Download the APK Watch the demo video
1

Allow the install

“File might be harmful” · “Not allowed to install unknown apps from this source”
  1. If Chrome warns about the file, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file and tap Install.
  3. If Android blocks it, tap Settings → turn on Allow from this source → go back → Install.
2

If Play Protect blocks it

“Blocked by Play Protect” · “Unsafe app blocked”
  1. Tap More details → Install anyway.
  2. No such option? Open the Play Store → your profile picture → Play Protect → gear icon → Pause or turn off Scan apps with Play Protect.
  3. Install the APK again, then switch scanning back on (Pause turns itself back on).
3

Allow SMS access (Android 13 and newer)

“Restricted setting: for your security, this setting is currently unavailable”
  1. In Paisa Pet, try allowing SMS once and tap OK on the message.
  2. Open Settings → Apps → Paisa Pet, tap ⋮ (top right) → Allow restricted settings, and confirm with your PIN.
  3. In Permissions, allow SMS and Notifications. Then go back to Paisa Pet.

If the ⋮ menu has no such option, look on the App info page itself. Screens differ slightly by phone brand.

Samsung: “Blocked by Auto Blocker”

Settings → Security and privacy → Auto Blocker → off. Install, then turn it back on.

Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme

Let the brand’s scan finish, then tap Install anyway. If prompts stop later, turn on Autostart and set battery to No restrictions.

“App not installed” or “parsing” error

Uninstall any older copy, re-download the file, and check your phone runs Android 8.0 or newer.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Does Paisa Pet use the internet?

No. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere. Everything is stored on your phone.

Which banks does it work with?

HDFC, SBI and ICICI UPI debit SMS are captured automatically, with a careful fallback for other banks. If a payment from your bank isn’t picked up, add it with + Add expense.

Does it read all my messages?

It only acts on bank UPI debit SMS. Personal messages, OTPs, credits and card spends are ignored, and message text is never saved.

Do I need to sign up?

No. There is no login and no account. Set a budget, allow two permissions, and you’re done in about two minutes.

Why does Android warn me when installing?

Because the app comes from a download link, not the Play Store. Play Protect hasn’t seen it before. The steps in the install section get you past each warning.

What if I don’t answer the notification?

The payment counts as Misc, so your “left” number never looks better than reality. You can change it later in Transactions.

Can I fix a wrong tag or add cash spends?

Yes. Edit or delete any payment in Transactions, and use + Add expense for cash or anything without an SMS.

Does it work on iPhone?

No. iOS doesn’t allow apps to read SMS, so Paisa Pet is Android only (Android 8.0 or newer).

Is it free?

Yes. The core app is free, with no ads, and your data is never sold.
